## What's working
1. **A striking visual plus a hard number.** The cottage reel ("23 cottages" in a pine forest) got about 35× the typical post.
2. **Hooks that name an objection or emotion.** The Noida comparison ("your family will ask questions") is the clear #2.
3. **Curiosity or exclusivity copy** ("Most people can't live here") beats plain feature statements.

## What's not working
- The small, thin, all-caps text on landscape shots (the Dholera pins, "Hotel managed", "Invest in peace") is pretty but has no hook, and it's unreadable at grid size. These sit around 300–500 views.
- White-background, text-only "episode" posts.
- **The three pinned posts are among the weakest.** Pin the 17.5K reel and the 2,689 carousel instead.
