### Location selling points
- Pangot sits at about 2,000 m altitude, 15 km (about 45 min) from Nainital town.
- It is about 310–320 km from Delhi (7–8 hr drive via Hapur, Moradabad, and Haldwani). The nearest rail is Kathgodam.
- The drive in passes through the **Naina Devi Bird Reserve**, Kilbury, and Snow View Point.
- It is one of India's top birding spots, with 580+ species recorded.
- The setting is oak, pine, and rhododendron forest with Himalayan views.
- Demand for second homes and holiday rentals in Nainital district (Mukteshwar, Pangot) keeps rising.

## 2. Dholera SIR, Gujarat

### Garg Realty in Dholera
- Committed ₹400 Cr+ over 3 years. The group holds about 20–22 acres already and is adding more land.
- **Sukkoon City** (Kasindra) and **Orchid Garden** (Pipli–Fedara Road) are the flagships and are villa-plot projects.
- Also listed: **Sukkoon Retreat** (Ratanpur) and **Sukkoon Premier**.
- Plot prices are not public (quotes come after an enquiry). **VERIFY** the price list, plot sizes, and RERA numbers per project.

### Dholera macro story (as of 2026)
- **Tata–PSMC semiconductor fab** (₹91,000 Cr+): structure complete, clean-room and tool install under way, trial output targeted for late 2026.
- **Tata semiconductor SEZ** (66 ha) notified in Apr 2026.
- **Dholera International Airport**: first trial landing by AAI on 4 Jun 2026, phase 1 targeted for Dec 2026.
- **Ahmedabad–Dholera Expressway** is operational.
- Over ₹2 lakh Cr in corporate commitments in the region.
